# The ledger model

A change of coordinates on the SBY bookkeeping that turns "beat Egan" into a
single scalar objective, plus the first computation of what the repo's own
lemmas permit at **n = 8**.

Everything here is generated by [`code/ledger.py`](https://github.com/levkropp/superperm/blob/main/code/ledger.py), which
re-derives it from `loop_runs.py` and asserts it against every walk the repo
has coordinates for.

---

## 1. The master identity

Put

$$d \;=\; v - (n-2)!$$

— the number of 2-loops entered **beyond an exact cover**. Substituting the
Split Identity $S = (n-1)(v-(n-2)!) - A$ into $T = S+B+Y$ gives

> $$\boxed{\;T \;=\; (n-1)\,d \;+\; (B+Y) \;-\; A\;}$$

This is an identity, not a bound. It says the three levers cost very
differently: an extra entered loop costs $n-1$, a block or a costly jump costs
$1$, and an **accident is a credit of $-1$**.

and the upstream clone (n = 5…9) and asserts the identity on each. **182
strings, identity holds on 182.** Records:

| Houston 872 | 6 | 872 | 29 | 29 | 5 | 0 | 25 | 4 | 0 | 4 | yes |
| champion 5906 | 7 | 5906 | 142 | 142 | 22 | 8 | 124 | 18 | 0 | **10** | yes |
| Raudvere 46204 | 8 | 46204 | 839 | 839 | 119 | 0 | 833 | 6 | 0 | **6** | yes |

Two corrections the census forced, both of which had been transcribed by hand
into an earlier draft:

* **Egan 5908 is not `(d=0, B=120, Y=24)`.** Measured, it is
  `(d=24, A=1, B=1, Y=0)` — a *single block*. That earlier row described the
  abstract complete-traversal construction, not the string.
* **The Egan vertex is uniform in $n$.** Every Egan string measured — 873,
  5908, 46205, 408966 — sits at exactly
  $A=1,\; B=1,\; Y=0,\; d=(n-3)!,\; S=(n-1)(n-3)!-1$, hence $B{+}Y{-}A = 0$.
* **409113 (n = 9) is the $d = 0$ vertex**, the first walk measured anywhere in

* **HPV-tightness is not necessary for optimality**: 4 of the 7 length-872

**The champion invariant, now measured not inferred.** All 169 length-5906
strings have $B{+}Y{-}A = 10$ exactly, while $A$ ranges over 8…16 and $S$ over

**The saving formula, checked at every record:**

In these coordinates the invariant is forced: HPV-tightness gives
$B{+}Y = 142-S$ and the Split Identity gives $A = 132-S$, so
$B{+}Y{-}A = 10$ identically. The individual coordinates scatter; the model's
objective does not. That is the evidence these are the right coordinates.

## 3. Egan is one point of this model

$$(n-2)! + (n-3)! \;=\; (n-1)\,(n-3)!$$

(144, 840, 5760 at $n = 7,8,9$). So the Egan bound is the zero-saving vertex
$d = (n-3)!$, $B{+}Y{-}A = 0$ — realised, as §2 measures, by
$A = 1, B = 1, Y = 0$: one block. The complete-traversal model
($d = 0$, $B = (n-2)!$, $Y \sim (n-3)!$) is a *different* vertex, realised by
409113. Neither is a rival framework; both are corners of this one — which is
why the Chain-Count Lemma, a theorem about the $d = 0$ corner, says nothing
about the rest.

**The objective.** Both champions are exactly HPV-tight ($T = v$). Imposing
that on the identity:

$$d \;=\; (n-3)! - \frac{B+Y-A}{n-2},
\qquad
\textbf{saving over Egan} \;=\; \frac{B+Y-A}{n-2}.$$

Verified: $n=6$, $B{+}Y{-}A = 4 \Rightarrow$ saving 1 (873 → 872). $n=7$,
$10 \Rightarrow$ saving 2 (5908 → 5906). $n=8$ record 46204 requires 6.

Two immediate consequences:

* **The exchange rate worsens with $n$** — $n-2$ units per character saved.
  There is no large-$n$ advantage in the objective itself.
* **The model is scale-free.** Saving a fraction $\varphi$ of $(n-3)!$ needs
  $B = \varphi\,(n-2)!$, and $B_{\max} = (n-2)!$. The required fraction of
  $B_{\max}$ is $\varphi$ at every $n$. So "go to larger $n$" is not a
  mechanism.

## 4. The HPV-tight frontier — computed

Define $d_{\min}(n)$ as the smallest $d$ at which a walk with $T = v$ is *not*
excluded by the arsenal (covering, absorption, the run cap, `dirty ≤ 2S`,
the loop-packing check, and the chain constraints (ii)+(iii)).

`ledger.py:tight_witness` decides this exactly. Two prunes make it fast:
$T \le v$ forces $N \le v+S$, a window of width $O((n-1)d)$ rather than
$O((n-1)!)$; and writing $E = (n-1)N - R$ for the unused arc capacity gives
$E = n_5 + (n-1)m - \text{short}$, so $n_5 \le E$ and $2m \le E$ and the
profile search is $O(E^2)$.

| $n$ | $d_{\min}$ | permitted saving $(n-3)!-d_{\min}$ | permitted length | truth (record) | model loose by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | 1 | 5 | 868 | 872 (saving 1) | ×5 |
| 7 | 1 | 23 | 5885 | 5906 (saving 2) | ×11.5 |
| 8 | **5** | 115 | 46090 | 46204 (saving 1) | ×115 |
| 9 | **> 14** (running) | < 706 | > 408260 | 408966 (saving 0) | — |

The $n=6$ and $n=7$ rows reproduce this repo's published ladder bounds (868,
5885) exactly, which is the gate.

**The new result is $d_{\min}(8) = 5$.** At $n = 6$ and $7$ the frontier sits
at $d = 1$; at $n = 8$ the four cheapest rungs are excluded outright. So the
model's own constraints *do* tighten with $n$. The mechanism is the dirty
budget: HPV-tightness forces $N \le v+S$, while the chain constraint forces
$Y$ up unless $\text{dirty} = 2S = 2(n-1)d$ is large, and those reconcile only
once $d$ is a constant fraction of $(n-3)!/(n-1)$. Witness at $n=8$, $d=5$:

```
A=0  S=35  N=738  f=712  n5=2  m=24  dirty=70  B=668  Y=22  T=725
```

**Ladder by-product.** Sweeping the cap gives the rung minimum $\min T(n,v)$,
so `ledger.py:ladder` recomputes the whole elementary ladder. It reproduces
every published rung as an assertion gate — $n=6$: 27, 20; $n=7$: **131, 121,
110, 105, 100** at $v = 120\ldots124$, matching `lemma_arsenal.md` §11 exactly
— and then gives $n=8$: 775, 763, 751, 738, 726, 713 at $v = 720\ldots725$.
The binding rung is $v = 725$ under HPV, so

$$s(8) \;\ge\; 45365 + 725 \;=\; 46090 .$$

This repo had never run the ladder at $n=8$. It is 13 below the Lean bound
46103 (the same shape as $n=7$, where the ladder gives 5885 against Lean's
5888), so it is an independent re-derivation, not a record.

## 4b. The Balance Bound — a closed form, and the property that grows with $n$

The exact-cover rung $v = (n-2)!$ is completely rigid: $R = (n-1)v = (n-1)!$
forces $A = 0$, $S = 0$, every loop fully used, $\text{dirty} \le 2S = 0$ and
$B = N$. Write $f$ for the number of complete traversals. Two lemmas already
in the arsenal then apply:

* **(runs)** a non-complete loop needs $\ge 2$ runs, so $N \ge 2v - f$;
* **(chain)** $f \le (n-2)(1+Y+N-f)$, so $Y \ge \frac{(n-1)f}{n-2} - N - 1$.

Hence $T = N+Y \ge \max\!\big(2v-f,\; \tfrac{(n-1)f}{n-2}-1\big)$. The first
falls and the second rises in $f$, so the bound is worst at the crossing:

> $$T \;\ge\; \left\lceil \frac{2(n-1)v - (n-2)}{2n-3} \right\rceil,
> \qquad
> g_0 \;:=\; T - v \;\ge\; \left\lceil \frac{(n-2)! - (n-2)}{2n-3} \right\rceil .$$

Pure algebra, no search. It reproduces the searched rung minima **exactly**:

| $n$ | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 12 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $T \ge$ | 7 | 27 | 131 | 775 | 5376 | 42692 | 3801600 |
| $g_0$ | 1 | 3 | 11 | 55 | 336 | 2372 | 172800 |
| $g_0/(n-3)!$ | .500 | .500 | .458 | .458 | .467 | .471 | .476 |

$n=5$ gives $146+7 = 153 = s(5)$ exactly (the soundness gate); $n=6,7,8$ match
the searched `min_T` (27, 131, 775) character for character.

**This is the property that appears at large $n$.** Since
$(n-2)!/(2n-3) \to (n-3)!/2$,

$$g_0 \;\sim\; \tfrac12 (n-3)!$$

— at the exact-cover rung the elementary bound already reaches **half of
Egan's entire excess**, for every $n$, with no computation. That is a
factorial-order lower bound above HPV, and it is not rigidity of a cover; it
is a balance between two monotone constraints in $f$.

## 4c. The Rung Bound — extending it off the exact-cover rung

The same balance runs at every rung, and this makes the collapse rate a
theorem rather than a fitted slope. At $v = (n-2)! + d$ with accidents $A$,
$S = (n-1)d - A$, and $u \le A$ loops not fully used:

* **(runs)** $N \ge 2v - f - u \ge 2v - f - A$;
* **(chain)** $f \le (n-2)(N-f+Y+\text{dirty}+1)$;
* **(dirty)** $\text{dirty} \le 2S$, and taking it maximal helps both.

Then $T = S + (N-\text{dirty}) + Y \ge \max\!\big(2v-f-A-S,\;
\tfrac{(n-1)f}{n-2}-3S-1\big)$, again falling and rising in $f$. At the
crossing the $A$-dependence cancels ($A$ survives only inside $f^\*$, which
decreases in $A$), so $A = 0$ is optimal and

> $$T \;\ge\; \left\lceil \frac{2(n-1)(n-2)! \;-\; (n-1)(4n-9)\,d \;-\;(n-2)}{2n-3}\right\rceil$$

with $d = 0$ recovering the Balance Bound. **The collapse rate is derived, not
fitted:**

$$\text{slope} \;=\; \frac{(n-1)(4n-9)}{2n-3} \;\sim\; 2n-3 .$$

It reproduces **all six** searched $n=8$ rungs exactly — 775, 763, 751, 738,
726, 713 — and is a valid (weaker) bound at $n = 6,7$, where the search's
extra packing constraints take over past $d = 2$.

Setting $T = v$ and solving gives the crossing in closed form:

> $$d^\* = \frac{(n-2)! - (n-2)}{(n-2)(4n-3)},
> \qquad
> s(n) \;\ge\; \mathrm{HPV}(n) + \lceil d^\* \rceil .$$

| $n$ | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 12 | 14 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $\lceil d^\*\rceil$ | 1 | 1 | 1 | 5 | 22 | 137 | 8,064 | 753,148 |
| bound | **153** | **868** | **5885** | **46090** | 408268 | 4032144 | 522555273 | 93885066759 |

The first four are exactly the searched values, and $n=5$ returns
$s(5) = 153$ — the soundness gate. The n = 9 sweep has excluded $d \le 15$ so
far, consistent with $d^\* = 22$; it is still running.

Asymptotically $d^\* \sim (n-2)!/(4n^2) = \Theta((n-4)!/4)$, so the ladder
delivers a bound sitting **factorially above HPV** — but a factor $\sim 4n$
below Egan's $(n-3)!$.

The collapse rate traces entirely to `dirty ≤ 2S`. So the gap between

* **HPV $+\ \Theta((n-3)!/2)$** — within a factor 2 of Egan — and
* **HPV $+\ \Theta((n-4)!/4)$** — what the ladder actually delivers —

**is exactly the `dirty ≤ 2S` leak, and nothing else.** That is the sharpest
available statement of §11, and it says the leak is now worth a factorial
factor, not a constant.

Read the last column. As the saving grows, blocks shorten toward exactly

saving every block *is* a complete traversal with $d = S = 0$, which is
precisely the configuration the Chain-Count Lemma excludes. **The model closes
on itself at the top: saving the full $(n-3)!$ is impossible.**

**The regime nobody has searched.** Every known champion sits at small $B$
($B = 4$ at $n=6$, $B = 18$ at $n=7$). Savings live at large $B$. Nothing in
this repo operates there — `loop_runs.py` and `a_split.py` are tuned to
$B \approx (n-2)!$ (Egan) or $B \approx 18$ (champion), and nothing sits at
$B = 360$.

## 5b. Where this sits against the published bounds — it does not win

Both Lean works are **general-n theorems**. Read from source:

| bound | excess over HPV | asymptotic |
|---|---|---|
| Hunter & Raudvere | $\dfrac{(n-2)!-(n-2)}{n^2-3n+1}$ | $\sim (n-4)!$ |
| Raudvere coeff2 | $\lceil((n-3)!-1)/(2n-1)\rceil$ | $\sim (n-4)!/2$ |
| **this note** | $\dfrac{(n-2)!-(n-2)}{(n-2)(4n-3)}$ | $\sim (n-4)!/4$ |

Hunter's formula reproduces all three published values exactly (869, 5888,
46103). **It has the same numerator as ours** — only the denominator differs,
$n^2-3n+1$ against $(n-2)(4n-3) = 4n^2-11n+6$, a factor $\to 4$. So we are
dominated at every $n \ge 6$ (tie at 6) and **never overtake**. Excesses:

| $n$ | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 11 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hunter | 2 | 4 | 18 | 92 | 568 | 4078 |
| coeff2 | 1 | 2 | 8 | 43 | 266 | 1920 |
| ours | 1 | 1 | 5 | 22 | 137 | 984 |

So §4c's $s(9) \ge 408268$ is an **independent re-derivation, not a result** —
Hunter already gives 408338.

**Where the factor 4 lives.** Redoing §4c with `dirty ≤ cS` as a parameter
gives denominator $(n-1)\big(c(3n-5)-(2n-3)\big)-1$, which is
$(n-2)(4n-3)$ at $c=2$ and **exactly Hunter's $n^2-3n+1$ at $c=1$**. But
`dirty ≤ S` is *false* — `lemma_arsenal.md` §8 retracts it, Houston 872 has
dirty = 49 against S = 25. So Hunter is not using a better dirty bound; he
recovers the factor another way, which localises our loss to the two
relaxations §4c actually makes: `N ≥ 2v − f − A`, and dropping the
$m$-refinement in the chain constraint.

**Consequence for the whole problem.** Every known lower bound is
$\Theta((n-4)!)$ and Egan is $\Theta((n-3)!)$. The ratio is $\Theta(n)$, the
absolute gap grows factorially, and nothing on either side closes it at any
$n$.

## 6. Status — what this is and is not

**Is:** an exact re-coordinatisation, validated on four walks and on the
136-champion invariant; a single scalar objective ($\max B{+}Y{-}A$); the
first $n=8$ numbers from this machinery; a precise parameter vector for any
target record.

**Is not:** a bound. The counting closure is loose by a factor of 5 at $n=6$,
11.5 at $n=7$, and 115 at $n=8$ — it permits 46090 at $n=8$, below the
machine-checked 46103. Feasibility in this model is a *necessary* condition
only; it does not imply constructibility.

**What would sharpen it:** the same lever the lower-bound side is stuck on
(`lemma_arsenal.md` §11) — coupling `dirty` to $N$ rather than bounding it by
$2S$. Every looseness above traces to `dirty ≤ 2S` being achievable in the
counting while `η = dirty/n_partial ≈ 0.98` is what real walks manage.
